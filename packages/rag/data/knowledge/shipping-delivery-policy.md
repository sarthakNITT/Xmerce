# Shipping & Delivery Policy

## Standard delivery

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. It is free on orders above INR 5,000. On orders of INR 5,000 or less, standard delivery costs INR 199.

## Express delivery

Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs INR 299 per order. Express delivery is offered for pincodes where it is available; the product page shows this when a pincode is entered.

## Order cancellation

An order can be cancelled before it ships. Open My Orders, choose the order and select Cancel order. Once the order status is Shipped it can no longer be cancelled, but it can be returned under the return policy.

## Delivery tracking

Every order moves through the statuses Confirmed, Packed, Shipped and Delivered. The current status and the courier tracking number are shown on the order detail page in My Orders.

## Delivery attempts

The courier makes up to three delivery attempts. If all three fail, the order is sent back to NovaTech and refunded as described in the Refund Policy.
