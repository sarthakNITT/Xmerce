# Store Operations Procedure

## Stock replenishment

Reorder a product when its stock cover is under 7 days. Stock cover is the units in stock divided by the average units sold per day over the last 14 days. The supplier lead time is 4 days.

## Discounts on slow-moving stock

Stock is slow-moving when it has more than 90 days of cover. Discounts on slow-moving stock are capped at 10% and need manager approval before they go live.

## Refund approvals

Refunds up to INR 5,000 are approved by a support executive. Refunds above INR 5,000 are approved by a manager.

## Agent controls

Store operations agents only propose changes to stock, price or money. Every proposal waits in the approval queue for a named approver. Staff can pause all agents, or one agent, at any time.
