# Coupon & Offer Terms

## General rules

Only one coupon can be used per order. Coupons cannot be combined with each other. A coupon is applied in the cart before payment and cannot be added after the order is placed.

## WELCOME5

WELCOME5 gives 5% off, up to a maximum discount of INR 5,000, on orders of INR 20,000 or more.

## FLAT1000

FLAT1000 gives INR 1,000 off on orders of INR 30,000 or more.

## Expired coupons

Expired coupons are rejected. The cart shows a message that the coupon has expired and the order total does not change.

## Minimum order value

The minimum order value is checked against the cart subtotal before delivery charges. If items are removed and the subtotal falls below the minimum, the coupon is removed from the cart.
