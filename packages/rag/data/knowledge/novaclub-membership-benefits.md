# NovaClub Membership Benefits

## About NovaClub

Every registered NovaTech customer is a NovaClub member. There are three tiers: Standard, Silver and Gold. New customers start at the Standard tier. The tier is shown on the customer profile.

## Standard tier

Standard members get free standard delivery on orders above INR 5,000 and earn 1x reward points on every purchase.

## Silver tier

Silver members get everything in the Standard tier. Silver adds priority support chat and 1.5x reward points on every purchase. Silver membership does not include free express delivery; express delivery is charged at the normal rate.

## Gold tier

Gold members get everything in the Silver tier. Gold adds free express delivery on every order, early access to sales and 2x reward points on every purchase.

## Reward points

Reward points are added to the account when an order is delivered. Points for a returned product are removed when the refund is issued.
