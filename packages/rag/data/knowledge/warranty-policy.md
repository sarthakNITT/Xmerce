# Warranty Policy

## Standard warranty

Laptops, desktops and all-in-ones carry a 1-year limited warranty from the date of delivery. The warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.

## Battery and charger

The battery and the charger carry a 6-month warranty from the date of delivery.

## Liquid and accidental damage

Liquid damage and accidental damage are not covered by the standard warranty. Liquid and accidental damage are covered only with the NovaCare 3-Year Plan.

## NovaCare 3-Year Plan

The NovaCare 3-Year Plan is an add-on that provides accidental damage cover for one laptop for three years from the date of delivery. It is bought together with the laptop and appears as a separate line in the order.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, cosmetic damage such as scratches, damage caused by unauthorised repair, or software problems caused by programs installed by the customer.

## How to claim

Raise a support ticket with the order number and a description of the problem. NovaTech support confirms whether the problem is covered and arranges a repair or replacement.
