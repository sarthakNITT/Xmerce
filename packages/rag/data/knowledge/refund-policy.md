# Refund Policy

## When a refund is issued

A refund is issued after the returned product passes inspection at the NovaTech returns centre, or when an order is cancelled before it ships.

## Timelines

Refunds go to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned product passes inspection. Cash-on-delivery refunds are paid by bank transfer within 7 to 10 days.

## Refund amount

The refund covers the price paid for the returned product, including tax. A coupon discount used on the order is not paid out in cash; the refund is the amount actually charged. The express delivery fee is refunded only when the product is returned because it is defective.

## Cash-on-delivery orders

For cash-on-delivery orders NovaTech needs the bank account details of the person who placed the order. The details are collected through a secure link sent when the return is approved.

## Refund status

The refund status is shown on the order detail page in My Orders. If a refund has not arrived after the timeline above, raise a support ticket with the order number.
