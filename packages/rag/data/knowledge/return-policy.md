# Laptop & Desktop Return Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to laptops, desktops and all-in-one PCs bought from the NovaTech online store in India. Add-ons such as the NovaCare 3-Year Plan follow the terms printed on the plan itself.

## Opened products

Opened laptops and desktops can be returned within 7 days of delivery if there is no physical damage and all accessories and the original box are included. The 7 days are counted from the delivery date shown in My Orders.

## Unopened products

Unopened products can be returned within 15 days of delivery. The factory seal must be intact and the original box must be undamaged.

## Custom-configured models

Custom-configured models can be returned only if they are defective. A model is custom-configured when the processor, memory, storage or colour was chosen on the product page and the unit was built to that order. A change of mind is not accepted as a reason to return a custom-configured model.

## How to raise a return

Open My Orders, choose the order and select Request a return. NovaTech arranges a free pickup from the delivery address. Keep the product, its accessories and the original box ready for the pickup agent.

## After the return is received

Each returned product is inspected at the NovaTech returns centre. If it passes inspection, the refund is issued as described in the Refund Policy. If it fails inspection, for example because of physical damage or missing accessories, the product is sent back to the customer.
